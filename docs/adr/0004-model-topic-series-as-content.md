# Model topic series as first-class content

Topic series are stored as their own Markdown collection, while Blog and AI entries reference a series and declare an explicit reading order. This gives each series a durable title, introduction, status, and landing page without copying articles or turning loose tags into navigation; tags remain descriptive metadata, and every article keeps one primary section and canonical URL.

**Status**: accepted

**Considered Options**: Generate series pages directly from tags; duplicate articles under a series directory; hard-code series on the homepage.
